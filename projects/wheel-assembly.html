Project 04 of 06 / Stallion Motorsport Electric

Wheel assembly: upright, hub and wheel centre

I designed the upright, hub and wheel centre for the lowest possible unsprung mass. The assembly is compact, uses aluminium alloys and is easy to put together and strip down.

The goal

The main goal was the lowest possible unsprung mass. I used aluminium alloys and kept the assembly compact.

Other requirements

  • Easy to assemble and strip down.
  • Strong enough for the loads on the vehicle.
  • Designed around the suspension parameters.

Analysis

I did the force analysis in Ansys.

Parts in the assembly

Upright, upper and lower shims, castle nut, hub, wheel centre and studs.
